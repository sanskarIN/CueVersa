# Public roadmap

Roadmap items are intentions, not delivery promises. Community feedback is
triaged through `docs/users_suggest/` and GitHub issues.

## Now — offline beta foundation

- Calibrate physics against documented real-table reference shots.
- Complete full-match device testing for 8-ball, 9-ball, AI, and local play.
- Replace scaffold launcher raster files with verified adaptive icon output.
- Complete English/Hindi human language review and TalkBack testing.
- Produce signed internal AAB and run Play pre-launch reports.

## Next — richer offline play

- Ball-in-hand placement UI and optional called-pocket selection.
- Straight-pool practice, time attack, target challenge, trick-shot packs, and
  replay/shot review.
- More cue/table cosmetics that preserve ball contrast and never change ranked
  physics.
- Versioned export/import with schema validation and integrity checks.

## Later — hosted online service

- Private friend room codes through an authoritative, versioned simulation
  service.
- Casual matchmaking, reconnect, abuse controls, and operational telemetry
  only after a published privacy review.
- Ranked play only after deterministic cross-runtime verification, server-side
  shot validation, fairness review, anti-cheat threat testing, and equal aim
  assistance.

Tournament brackets, ads, billing, cloud sync, and hosted leaderboards remain
future proposals. They are not present or implied by the current app.
