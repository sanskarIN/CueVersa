# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CueVerse harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, patient, and open to correction.
- Give actionable feedback about work, never personal attacks.
- Respect accessibility needs, cultural context, privacy, and consent.
- Credit ideas and assets accurately and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Accept moderator decisions intended to protect the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized attention, doxxing, spam,
impersonation, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and
encouraging others to violate these rules are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report community conduct concerns privately to
`supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, temporarily suspend
participation, or permanently ban severe/repeated violations. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed where safety or law requires escalation.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1. Community Impact
Guidelines inform proportionate enforcement.
