# Haptics

Haptics are enabled by default and optional. A human shot uses a light impact;
AI and passive simulation do not vibrate. Turning haptics off suppresses app
requests immediately.

Future patterns may distinguish legal placement, warning, and achievement, but
must remain sparse, never encode sole meaning, respect Android/system policy,
and be tested on representative devices. No repeated vibration during rolling,
aim dragging, advertisements, or support prompts.
