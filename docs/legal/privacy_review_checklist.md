# Privacy and legal publication checklist

- [ ] Developer/legal identity, contact, jurisdiction, address, tax, and store
  details are supplied and verified; none are invented.
- [ ] Data map matches actual release traffic, SDKs, permissions, and storage.
- [ ] Store data-safety form matches the app and privacy policy.
- [ ] Children/family, consumer, accessibility, export, sanctions, ads, billing,
  and contest/promotion rules reviewed for target markets.
- [ ] Retention/deletion/access processes are executable and tested.
- [ ] Terms, privacy, licenses, notices, asset provenance, and support claims are
  reviewed by qualified counsel where needed.
- [ ] BMC wording remains optional and makes no guaranteed-return promise.
- [ ] Rule profiles do not falsely claim official governing-body status.
- [ ] Version/effective dates and change notification method are finalized.
