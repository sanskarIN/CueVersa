# Future premium decision gate

Premium remains a proposal. Before implementation answer:

1. What durable user value exists without making free core gameplay worse?
2. Is every ranked mechanic identical and every paid advantage absent?
3. Are prices, recurring terms, trial, tax, cancellation, refunds, restore, and
   family purchase protections clear before confirmation?
4. Which entitlement is client-cached and which server-verified? How do offline,
   reinstall, refund, chargeback, account deletion, and service sunset behave?
5. Does Apache-2.0 redistribution remain accurately explained?
6. Are receipts/tokens excluded from logs and preferences and handled through
   platform-compliant billing/security?
7. Have accessibility, localization, privacy, security, store policy, legal,
   support load, and failure paths passed review?

No implementation begins until the answers are documented and approved.
