# BMC support policy

Destination: `https://buymeacoffee.com/sanskarIN`.

Support is voluntary, external, and unrelated to match results, unlocks,
feature votes, deadlines, warranties, ownership, investment returns, or support
priority. CueVerse does not record link clicks or donation status.

The card uses original CueVerse imagery, a clear external-link icon, accessible
label, localized disclaimer, large target, launch-failure feedback, and no
gameplay popup. It may appear on Home, Settings, Support, About, repository/docs,
and reasonable release-note footers.

Any future supporter recognition requires explicit opt-in, content moderation,
privacy/retention review, revocation behavior, and no competitive benefit.
