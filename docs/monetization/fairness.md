# Competitive fairness

Ranked physics, legal rules, input sampling, shot power limits, aim guide,
collision tolerances, timer, and reconnect policy must be equal for all players.
Paid cues and effects are cosmetic in ranked play. If casual statistics ever
exist, ranked play normalizes them completely and states that behavior clearly.

Never sell longer trajectory prediction, improved collision outcomes, hidden
information, favorable matchmaking, result reversal, extra ranked time, or
server authority. Never change odds, price, difficulty, or offers secretly per
person. No fake scarcity, fake discounts, forced continuity, obstructed cancel,
or disguised advertising.

Fairness changes require deterministic A/B prohibition in ranked mode, rules
documentation, replay comparison, economy review, accessibility check, and
public release notes.
