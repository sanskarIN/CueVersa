# Play Store pre-release

- Current target API/deadline, 64-bit ABI, app bundle, signing, package ownership.
- Original icon, feature graphic, screenshots, short/full descriptions, title,
  category, contact, privacy URL, open-source/support disclosures.
- Accurate data safety, permissions, ads, content rating, target audience,
  families, billing/subscription, accessibility, and account-deletion forms.
- No trademark confusion, copied assets, fake gameplay, misleading “online” or
  “official rules,” fake discount, gambling/wagering, or unimplemented feature.
- Internal → closed → open/production staged testing with pre-launch report,
  crash/ANR/device catalog review, support owner, monitoring, halt/rollback.
- Country/age/legal/tax/merchant details supplied by the publisher and legally
  reviewed; repository templates do not invent them.

Always follow current Play Console policy at release time; this checklist is not
a substitute for current official requirements.
