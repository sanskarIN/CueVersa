# Detailed roadmap

## Offline beta

Finish control/rule UX, device QA, adaptive icon/splash resources, calibration,
accessibility audits, localization review, export/import design, and signed
internal distribution.

## Content beta

Add straight-pool practice, target challenge, time attack, solo score, curated
trick shots, replay/review, richer tutorial, persistent achievements/missions,
and original cosmetic packs.

## Private online preview

Build an isolated authoritative service, protocol/version negotiation, friend
room codes, reconnect, moderation/reporting, privacy/retention controls,
observability, rate limiting, load tests, and region/security review.

## Competitive evaluation

Only after private service maturity: casual matchmaking, then opt-in ranked
evaluation with equal aim help, normalized cosmetics, cross-runtime
determinism, anti-cheat, appeal/abuse handling, and public fairness rules.

## Long term

Tournament brackets, optional hosted premium, sponsorship, additional
platforms, and community-authored challenge tooling subject to security,
license, moderation, accessibility, and sustainable operations.
