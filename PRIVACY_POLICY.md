# CueVerse privacy policy (developer-prepared template)

Effective draft date: 2026-08-14. **Legal review is required before commercial
publication.** No unprovided legal entity, address, registration, tax identity,
or jurisdiction is asserted by this template.

## Current app behavior

CueVerse stores settings, tutorial/progress state, local statistics, and
offline game state on the user's device. The current open-source build does not
include accounts, a backend, analytics, advertising, purchases, cloud saves,
leaderboards, push notifications, or crash-report uploads. Optional support,
GitHub, and email actions open an external application only after the user taps
the corresponding control.

CueVerse does not record whether a user opened the Buy Me a Coffee link.
External services and email applications apply their own privacy terms once
opened.

## Data stored locally

- language, theme, accessibility, sound/haptics, control, and performance
  preferences;
- XP, level calculation inputs, matches, wins, streak, shots, pots, and derived
  accuracy;
- developer-options unlock and safe local diagnostic preferences.

No password, payment credential, precise location, contact list, microphone,
camera, advertising identifier, or biometric data is requested by CueVerse.

## Control and retention

Local data remains until the app's delete/reset controls are used, Android
clears application storage, or the app is uninstalled. Settings provides a
confirmed “Delete all local data” action. Android cloud backup/device transfer
is disabled for preferences and databases in this build.

## Children and families

The app contains no wagering, cash-out currency, loot boxes, or behavioral ads.
Store distribution still requires an age/family-policy review appropriate to
the selected markets.

## Future changes

Online multiplayer, cloud sync, analytics, ads, purchases, crash reporting, or
notifications must not launch until this policy and `docs/privacy/data_map.md`
identify the exact data, purpose, recipient, retention, legal basis where
applicable, and user controls. Material changes require a new effective date.
